# Writing in the quiet hours

The first light reaches my desk before the rest of the day arrives. There is a cup of coffee, a page waiting to be filled, and enough room for one good thought.

## A small ritual

A useful draft begins with **something worth noticing**. I collect a few lines, give them a heading, and let the shape of the article emerge.

> Make room for the sentence you have not written yet.

- [x] Find a quiet place to begin
- [x] Put the first thought on the page
- [ ] Follow it into a finished story

## From thought to post

| Stage | A little attention |
| --- | --- |
| Gather | Notes, links, and a few details |
| Write | One paragraph at a time |
| Publish | A post that feels like you |

A blog is a place to return to. Some days a paragraph is enough; other days the words keep coming.
